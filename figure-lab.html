Spec

JSON. Expressions use calculator syntax: exp(-t)*cos(3t), u(t-2), sqrt(x), pi. Number fields also take constants in quotes: "pi/2", "2pi", "e".

Shared fields
  • kind: "graph", "region", or "phase"; from: the equation on the page (required)
  • window: {"x": [a, b], "y": [c, d]}; either may be left out for automatic (graphs need x)
  • axes: {"x": "t", "y": "y"}; the independent axis's label is the variable when it is one letter (else set var)
  • points: {"at": [x, y]} or {"on": {"curve": …, "at": …}}, with optional label, fill ("closed"/"open"), pos ("above"/"below"/"left"/"right")
  • labels: {"at": [x, y], "text": …}; ticks: {"x": [0, {"at": 3.14159265, "label": "π"}]}
  • Functions: sin cos tan sec csc cot asin acos atan sinh cosh tanh exp ln log (natural) log10 log2 sqrt cbrt abs sign floor ceil min max, and u/H (unit step, 1 at 0).
Graph fields
  • curves: {"expr": …, "label": …, "style": "solid" | "dashed" | "dotted"} or {"pieces": [{"expr": …, "on": [a, b], "ends": ["closed", "open"]}]}
  • impulses: {"at": x, "size": 1, "label": "δ(t − 2)"}; points "on": {"curve": 1, "at": x}
Region fields
  • Vertical slices: top, bottom (default 0), as expressions in x. Horizontal slices: right, left (default 0) in y, or set "dir": "dy".
  • A boundary may be {"expr": …, "label": "y = √x"}.
  • between: [a, b], or "intersections" (default)
  • slice: {"at": 0.5, "label": "Δx", "width": 0.05}; markIntersections: true; area: true puts the area in the alt text
  • Points "on": {"curve": "top", "at": x}
Phase-plane fields
  • system: {"x": "y", "y": "-2x - 3y"}, keyed by the axis variables (no t), or A: [[0, 1], [-2, -3]]
  • vars: ["x", "y"] when the axis labels aren’t single letters; one-character labels such as θ are used in the text
  • show: any of "field", "equilibria" (default: these two), "nullclines", "eigenvectors"
  • trajectories: {"start": [x, y], "direction": "both" | "forward" | "backward", "label": …}
  • labelEquilibria: true writes their coordinates; window may be left out (centred on the equilibria, nearly equal scales)
