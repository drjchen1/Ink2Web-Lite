Spec

Accepts Line-DSL (v2) or legacy JSON (v1). Expressions accept native LaTeX (\frac{1}{x}, \sqrt{x}, u_c(t), \begin{cases}...\end{cases}) as well as calculator syntax (exp(-t)*cos(3t), pi/2).

Line-DSL (v2) quick reference
  • Header: scene: plot | diagram | image (plot is default), from: ... (required), space: cartesian | polar | complex | 1d, schematic: true, param: c = 2
  • Axes & ticks: axes: t [0, 5], y [-1, 3] (omit brackets for automatic range), tick-x: pi:"\pi", 2pi, tick-y: ...
  • Curves: curve: \frac{\sin x}{x} | form=y | dashed | domain=[0, 2pi] | ends=[closed, open] | label="f(x)" | guides; supports form=polar|parametric|implicit|x and \begin{cases}...\end{cases}
  • Shading & Riemann: shade: \sqrt{x} .. x^2 | on=intersections | slice=0.55:"\Delta x" | riemann=6:midpoint | area; complex ROC: roc: Re(s) > -2
  • Fields & flows: field: slope t - y | field | isoclines or field: vector y ; -2x - 3y | field | equilibria | nullclines | eigenvectors; flow: [2, 0] | forward
  • Marks: point: [c, 1] | open | label="..." | pos=above, pole: [-2, 2], zero: [-1, 0], impulse: 2 | size=1, arrow: [0,0] -> [2,0] | dashed, sign: [-inf, 1]:-, interval: (1, 4]
  • Diagrams (scene: diagram): node: g = box("G(s)") @ (1, 0) (shapes: box, circle, sum, mass, wall, tank, ground, point), edge: r -> g | arrow | label="E(s)" (styles: arrow, line, dashed, spring, damper)
Legacy JSON (v1) fields
  • kind: "graph", "region", "phase", "slope", or "line"; from: the equation on the page (required)
  • window: {"x": [a, b], "y": [c, d]}; axes: {"x": "t", "y": "y"}; points, labels, ticks
  • curves, impulses (graph); top, bottom, right, left, between, slice, markIntersections, area (region)
  • system or A, vars, show, trajectories, labelEquilibria (phase); slope, solutions (slope); intervals, signs, poles, zeros, roc (line)
